Honey The Corgi

Meet the lady!

Honey is a Pembroke Welsh Corgi, born March 3rd, 2026. Strange that the all American Cleatus got such a British dog, but when he saw her for the first time, he knew he'd love her forever!

She Enjoys: long walks, meeting people, peanut butter, chicken over beef, bones, sniffing a single leaf for 20-minutes, barking, peeing on Cleatus's carpet, chewing Cleatus's walls, shoes, & socks.

She Hates: the vaccume cleaner, Cleatus's Banjo, not getting a piece of your food, and being told no.

Dispite her imperfections, Cleatus still loves her very dearly.
